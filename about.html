About Us

About Sobha One World

Sobha Limited built the premium private estate project Sobha One World in Hoskuru, Hoskote, East Bangalore. The main area of the project is over 350 acres, and the first part of Sobha One Residences takes up 48 acres. The huge project has 6 beautiful towers, each with 46 floors. It has 3484 high-end flats that are Vaastu-compliant and designed for city life. These big flats come in different sizes, from 740 to 2425 square feet and are called 1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK, and 4BHK. This project costs ₹14,720 per square foot. The price for a 1 BHK will likely start at ₹ 1.09 Cr before the launch. The project is set to begin in earnest in May 2026.

Legacy of Sobha Limited

Sobha Limited is one of India’s most reputed and trusted real estate developers, known for its commitment to quality construction and timely delivery. Established in 1995 by P.N.C. Menon, the company has grown into a major national developer with a strong presence across India.

Over the years, Sobha Limited has delivered hundreds of landmark residential and commercial developments, earning a reputation for precision engineering and premium craftsmanship.

  • Over 576 completed projects
  • More than 148+ million sq. ft. developed
  • Presence across 27 cities in 14 states
  • Over 30 years of industry experience

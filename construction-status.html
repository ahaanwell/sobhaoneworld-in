Sobha One World Construction Status

Sobha One World Construction Status

The construction status of Sobha One World is currently in the pre-launch phase as of May 2026. The project is strategically located in Hoskote, East Bangalore, and the official launch is scheduled for 20 May 2026. While full-scale construction has not yet started, initial site development activities are already in progress.

Sobha Limited is actively working on securing all necessary approvals, including Karnataka RERA registration. Once approvals are obtained, the main construction work will begin as per the planned timeline.

This township spans 300 acres, featuring 18 high-rise towers with 53 floors each, and includes approximately 5,406 premium apartments, making it one of the largest residential developments in East Bangalore.

Current Construction Milestone Overview

During the pre-launch phase, early buyers can benefit from exclusive pricing advantages, with discounts of up to 20% on selected units. Prices are expected to increase by approximately 5% after the official launch on 20 May 2026.

Key Project Milestones:

  • Pre-launch Date: 10 May 2026
  • Launch Date: 20 May 2026
  • Project Duration: 5 Years
  • Completion Date: 20 December 2031
  • Possession Date: 31 December 2031

These milestones provide a clear timeline for buyers, helping them plan their investment and monitor construction progress effectively. Sobha Limited’s track record of delivering 500+ projects across India adds further confidence in timely completion.

Expected Possession and RERA Approval

The RERA approval for Sobha One World is expected around May 2026, close to the launch timeline. Once approved, the official registration number will be available on the Karnataka RERA portal, allowing buyers to verify all project-related details.

The possession timeline is set for 31 December 2031, aligning with a structured 5-year construction schedule. Given Sobha’s strong history of timely delivery, this timeline is considered realistic and dependable.

Tower-Wise Construction Progress

The project will be developed in multiple phases. Phase 1, known as Sobha One Residences, covers 48 acres and includes all 18 towers with a 3 Basement + Ground + 53-floor structure.

Detailed tower-wise construction updates will be shared after the official launch. These updates will help buyers track individual tower progress and plan their move-in timeline more efficiently.

Excavation and Foundation Work

Sobha One World is currently in the planning and approval stage. The RERA application has already been submitted, which is a critical legal step ensuring compliance with government regulations and buyer protection.

After the project launch, excavation and foundation work will begin. This phase marks the first major stage of physical construction.

Sobha Limited uses its in-house construction model, which enables faster execution and better quality control compared to developers relying on third-party vendors.

Given the scale of 5,406 units across 18 towers, the foundation stage will be a major engineering milestone. Regular updates will be provided as work progresses.

Project Readiness and Planning

Sobha Limited is known for its detailed pre-construction planning approach. For Sobha One World, all major aspects such as master planning, tower design, and unit layouts are finalized before launch.

Available Unit Configurations:

  • 1 BHK: 740 sq. ft.
  • 2 BHK: 1,070 sq. ft.
  • 2 BHK (Large): 1,200 sq. ft.
  • 3 BHK Lux: 1,525 sq. ft.
  • 3 BHK Grande (2 Car Park): 1,825 sq. ft.
  • 4 BHK Lux (2 Car Park): 2,100 sq. ft.
  • 4 BHK Grande (2 Car Park): 2,500 sq. ft.

Model apartments for all configurations will be available at the site after the official launch. Finalizing layouts in advance ensures better execution and reduces construction delays.

Monthly Site Progress Updates

Sobha Limited maintains transparency by sharing regular construction updates. Monthly site progress photos will be published on official platforms, along with updates through brochures and sales channels.

Sobha One World is progressing in a structured and well-planned manner. With approvals underway and launch approaching, the project is set to move smoothly into the construction phase.

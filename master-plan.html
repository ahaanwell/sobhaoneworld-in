Master Plan

Sobha OneWorld Master Plan

The Sobha OneWorld master plan provides a complete overview of how this large-scale residential township is thoughtfully designed and developed. It gives a clear picture of how different elements such as apartment towers, internal roads, landscaped areas, amenities, and open spaces are organized across the project.

Phase 1 of Sobha OneWorld is spread across 48 acres, forming the foundation of a much larger township planned over 300 acres in East Bangalore. The master plan is especially helpful for buyers as it allows them to visualize the exact placement of homes, understand the surroundings, and choose the right unit based on location, view, and convenience.

This township is designed with a modern urban lifestyle in mind, focusing on comfort, space, and long-term livability. The layout ensures that residents experience a well-balanced environment where nature and infrastructure coexist harmoniously.

Why the Sobha OneWorld Master Plan is Important

A well-designed master plan is the backbone of any large residential project. It helps buyers understand the overall vision and future growth of the township.

The Sobha OneWorld master plan clearly explains:

  • The positioning of residential towers
  • The layout of internal roads and pathways
  • The distribution of green spaces and parks
  • The placement of lifestyle amenities
  • The planning of future development phases

By studying the master plan PDF, buyers can make smarter decisions about which tower, floor level, or facing direction suits their needs best.

Open Spaces and Green Living

One of the most attractive features of Sobha OneWorld is its emphasis on open and green spaces. Nearly 80% of the total area is reserved for open zones, making it a low-density and spacious community.

These open areas include:

  • Beautifully landscaped gardens
  • Tree-lined walking and jogging tracks
  • Dedicated play areas for children
  • Quiet seating zones for relaxation
  • Community interaction spaces

Because the buildings occupy only a smaller portion of the land, residents can enjoy a peaceful environment with fresh air, natural light, and greenery all around. This kind of planning is ideal for families, working professionals, and senior citizens.

Project Phases and Development Timeline

  • Phase 1 Area: 48 acres
  • Number of Towers in Phase 1: 18
  • Current Status: Pre-launch stage
  • Expected Possession: December 2031

This phased approach allows the developer to focus on construction quality while also ensuring timely delivery.

Key Highlights of Sobha OneWorld Master Plan

  • Total Township Area: 300 acres

  • Total Units: Approximately 5,406 apartments

  • Clubhouse: Large central lifestyle hub
  • RERA Status: Approval awaited

Tower Planning and Layout

The tower layout at Sobha OneWorld is designed to provide a premium high-rise living experience. The towers are strategically placed with sufficient distance between them to avoid congestion.

  • Maximum natural sunlight inside homes
  • Proper cross ventilation
  • Reduced noise levels
  • Enhanced privacy for residents

The vertical design also offers stunning views of the surrounding cityscape, making the living experience more premium.

Apartment Configurations and Sizes

  • 1 BHK: 740 sq ft
  • 2 BHK: 1,070 – 1,200 sq ft
  • 3 BHK: 1,525 – 1,825 sq ft
  • 4 BHK: 2,100 – 2,500 sq ft

Each apartment is designed with a focus on functionality, space utilization, and natural lighting. Homes are also planned according to Vaastu principles, making them more appealing to Indian buyers.

Clubhouse and Lifestyle Planning

The clubhouse at Sobha OneWorld acts as the central hub for recreation and social interaction. It is strategically located so that residents from all towers can access it easily.

Spread across 50,000+ sq ft, the clubhouse is designed like a luxury resort and offers a wide range of amenities.

Clubhouse Features Include:

  • Swimming pool and kids’ pool
  • Fully equipped modern gym
  • Yoga and meditation zones
  • Indoor games and activity rooms
  • Library and reading spaces
  • Banquet hall and party areas
  • Café and dining spaces
  • Mini theatre
  • Children’s play zones
  • Spa and wellness facilities

Safety and Sustainability Features

Security Features:

  • 24/7 security personnel
  • CCTV surveillance in common areas
  • Controlled entry and exit points

Sustainability Features:

  • Rainwater harvesting systems
  • Use of treated water for landscaping

  • Proper waste management systems

Additionally, the project includes vehicle-free podium areas, creating a safer and quieter environment for residents, especially children and elderly people.

FAQs – Sobha OneWorld Master Plan

1. What information is included in the Sobha OneWorld master plan?

The Sobha OneWorld master plan provides a complete overview of the township layout. It shows the exact placement of residential towers, internal roads, landscaped gardens, open areas, clubhouse, and other key amenities. This helps buyers clearly visualize how the project is structured before visiting the site or making a purchase decision.

2. What is the total size of Phase 1 in Sobha OneWorld?

Phase 1 of Sobha OneWorld is developed over 48 acres of land. It forms a part of a much larger 300-acre integrated township, which will be developed in multiple phases. This large-scale planning makes it one of the most expansive residential projects in East Bangalore.

3. How much open space is planned in Sobha OneWorld?

Approximately 80% of the total project area is dedicated to open spaces. These include landscaped gardens, parks, walking tracks, and green zones. Since only a limited portion of land is used for construction, residents can enjoy a more peaceful, airy, and nature-friendly living environment.

4. How many towers and apartments are included in the project?

According to the master plan, Phase 1 consists of 18 high-rise towers with around 5,406 apartments. The project offers a wide range of configurations including 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK homes, making it suitable for different types of buyers and family sizes.

5. Are amenities and clubhouse included in the master plan?

Yes, the master plan includes a centrally located large clubhouse along with multiple modern amenities. Facilities such as a swimming pool, gym, indoor games area, children’s play zones, landscaped walking tracks, and relaxation spaces are thoughtfully placed to ensure easy accessibility for residents from all towers.

East Bangalore

East Bangalore has emerged as one of the most promising regions for property investment in 2026. With rapid infrastructure development and strong demand, large township projects like Sobha One World — spread across 300 acres—are contributing significantly to this growth.

For homebuyers searching for apartments in East Bangalore, the area stands out due to consistent price appreciation and attractive rental returns. In particular, locations around Hoskote are gaining attention as they offer better long-term potential compared to already saturated areas.

Why East Bangalore is a Preferred Location in 2026

The region’s popularity is driven by its proximity to major employment hubs such as Whitefield and the Hoskote Industrial Area. This has led to an increase in job opportunities and demand for residential spaces.

Strong Connectivity & Infrastructure

Road networks like NH 75 and the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) have significantly improved travel convenience. The Purple Line Metro extension (Kadugodi station) further enhances connectivity, allowing easy access to key parts of the city. The Kempegowda International Airport can be reached within 35–45 minutes.

Access to Education

Families benefit from reputed schools and colleges nearby. Institutions like Inventure Academy and MVJ College of Engineering are easily accessible, making the location suitable for long-term living.

Everyday Convenience

Shopping centres such as Orion Uptown Mall and local retail hubs ensure that daily needs are easily met. Many townships also include internal commercial spaces for added convenience.

Safe & Spacious Living

Modern gated communities in Hoskote offer secure environments with 24/7 surveillance and ample green spaces, providing a balanced lifestyle away from city congestion.

Sobha OneWorld – Township Living in Hoskote

Sobha OneWorld is a large-scale residential development in East Bangalore, featuring over 5,400 apartments across 18 high-rise towers. Designed as a self-sustained township, it combines residential, commercial, and lifestyle spaces within one community.

Compared to areas like Whitefield, property prices here remain relatively affordable, making it attractive for both end-users and investors.

Pricing & Project Overview (2026)

The current price is approximately ₹12,600 per sq. ft., offering competitive value in the East Bangalore market. The project is in its early development phase and has applied for RERA approval.

Booking & Possession

Buyers can secure a unit with an initial 10% payment. Possession is expected by December 2031, with preliminary site work already underway.

Construction Quality

Sobha is known for its in-house construction capabilities, including material production and finishing work, which helps maintain consistent quality and timely delivery.

Connectivity & Future Growth Potential

Hoskote’s appeal lies in its excellent connectivity to major road networks such as the STRR and the upcoming Bangalore–Chennai Expressway. Key employment zones like Marathahalli and the Hoskote Industrial Area are within easy reach.

Growth Prospects

Areas near Budigere Cross are witnessing rapid development due to upcoming infrastructure projects like the Peripheral Ring Road (PRR). This is expected to boost property values in the coming years.

Rental Demand

Due to proximity to IT hubs, rental demand remains strong. Monthly rents in this region typically range from ₹35,000 to ₹45,000 depending on the unit type and amenities.

FAQs

Why is East Bangalore a good investment option in 2026?

East Bangalore is witnessing strong growth due to expanding IT hubs, improved road networks like STRR, and metro connectivity. These developments are increasing housing demand and supporting steady property price appreciation.

When is the expected possession date for Sobha OneWorld?

The project is scheduled for completion by December 2031. As it is currently in the early stages, buyers have the advantage of entering at relatively lower prices.

What is the distance from Sobha OneWorld to Whitefield and the airport?

Sobha OneWorld is approximately 12–13 km from Whitefield, with a travel time of around 20–25 minutes. The Kempegowda International Airport is about 28–30 km away and can be reached in roughly 35–45 minutes, depending on traffic.

Is Hoskote suitable for gated community living?

Hoskote is becoming a preferred location for gated communities due to its peaceful environment, availability of open spaces, and improving infrastructure. It also offers access to essential services such as schools, hospitals, and workplaces.

What future developments are planned near Budigere Cross?

The area is set to benefit from major infrastructure projects like the Peripheral Ring Road and the Bangalore–Chennai Expressway. These developments are expected to enhance connectivity and contribute to long-term property value growth.

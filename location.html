Sobha OneWorld Location

Sobha OneWorld enjoys a prime location in Hoskote, positioned along the highly active Old Madras Road (NH-75), directly opposite the Hoskote Toll Plaza in East Bangalore, Karnataka – 562114. This strategic address places the project within one of the fastest-developing corridors of the city. Spread across a massive 300-acre integrated township, with Phase 1 covering 48 acres, the development stands out as a preferred destination for modern homebuyers looking for both convenience and long-term value.

One of the biggest advantages of this location is its excellent connectivity to major IT and business hubs in East Bangalore. Key employment zones such as Whitefield (approximately 11 km), ITPL (around 15 km), Bagmane Tech Park (17 km), Laptech IT Services (10 km), and RMZ Ecospace (18 km) are easily accessible from the project. This makes it an ideal residential choice for working professionals, especially those in the IT sector, as it significantly reduces daily commute time and helps maintain a better work-life balance.

Hoskote has rapidly evolved into a promising real estate destination due to continuous infrastructure growth and increasing demand. The area is gaining attention for its future potential, supported by major developments like the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) and the Bangalore–Chennai Expressway (NE7). These infrastructure projects are expected to improve connectivity further and boost property appreciation over time.

Unlike the crowded core areas of Bangalore, Hoskote offers a more open and peaceful environment. The locality features wider roads, less congestion, and cleaner surroundings, making it attractive for families seeking a calm yet well-connected lifestyle. This balance between urban access and suburban comfort is one of the key reasons why more buyers are choosing this region over densely populated city zones.

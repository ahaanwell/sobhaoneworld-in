Sobha One World Amenities

Sobha One World is designed as a premium residential township that offers an elevated lifestyle with over 55 world-class amenities thoughtfully planned across a sprawling 48-acre development (Phase 1). The project focuses on providing residents with a perfect blend of comfort, recreation, and convenience within a well-planned environment.

Residents can experience a wide range of lifestyle features, including a luxurious clubhouse, multiple swimming pools, well-equipped sports courts, landscaped gardens, and dedicated spaces for relaxation and fitness. In addition to leisure facilities, the township also integrates modern conveniences such as a retail plaza and co-working zones along Old Madras Road, allowing residents to enjoy a self-sustained living experience without needing to travel far for daily needs.

The development comprises 18 high-rise towers, each built with a structure of three basement levels, a ground floor, and 53 upper floors. Altogether, the project offers approximately 5,406 thoughtfully designed apartments. These homes are available in 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, and 4 BHK configurations, catering to a wide range of homebuyers—from individuals and young professionals to large families.

Apartment sizes range from around 740 sq. ft. to 2,500 sq. ft., ensuring flexibility in choosing a home that matches both lifestyle preferences and budget. With its extensive amenities, smart planning, and focus on safety and comfort, Sobha One World stands out as one of the most desirable residential projects in Hoskote, offering a modern and well-balanced way of living.

Sobha One World Amenities – A Complete Lifestyle Experience

Sobha One World is thoughtfully designed to offer a well-balanced lifestyle with a wide range of premium amenities catering to relaxation, fitness, recreation, and everyday convenience. Spread across a beautifully planned township, these facilities create a vibrant and comfortable living environment for residents of all age groups.

Recreational Spaces for Relaxation & Leisure

The recreational zone at Sobha One World is designed to help residents unwind and spend quality time with family and friends. It offers a mix of indoor and outdoor leisure options that encourage both relaxation and social interaction.

Residents can enjoy indoor activities such as board games, snooker, squash, and other engaging options within dedicated spaces. Wellness-focused amenities like a spa, sauna, jacuzzi, and meditation areas provide the perfect setting to relax and rejuvenate.

The township also features a grand clubhouse, scenic bio-pond, bonfire court, and open spaces for casual gatherings. Outdoor courts and activity zones add to the lively atmosphere, making it easy to stay entertained within the community.

Sports & Fitness Facilities

For those who enjoy an active lifestyle, Sobha One World offers a wide range of sports and fitness amenities. The project includes tree-lined jogging and walking tracks that allow residents to stay fit while enjoying a peaceful environment.

Sports enthusiasts can make use of facilities such as cricket practice nets, a golf putting zone, basketball courts, and multi-purpose sports areas. Additional features like beach volleyball courts, skating rinks, and outdoor exercise decks further enhance the fitness experience.

Children and adults alike can enjoy open playgrounds, cycling paths, and a large outdoor swimming pool, ensuring there is something for everyone to stay active and healthy.

Lifestyle Amenities for Modern Living

The township goes beyond basic facilities by offering modern lifestyle amenities designed to enhance daily living. Residents have access to tennis courts, futsal grounds, and volleyball courts for recreational sports.

Unique features such as urban farming zones and community gardens encourage sustainable living and community interaction. Social spaces like poolside pavilions, gathering areas, and cabanas provide ideal settings for relaxation and celebrations.

Additional attractions include an infinity-edge swimming pool, pet park, adventure play areas, giant chess boards, and dedicated skate parks. Well-planned children’s play zones ensure that younger residents also have safe and engaging spaces to enjoy.

Premium Clubhouse Experience

Sobha One World features multiple clubhouses designed to deliver a luxury lifestyle experience. The development includes two expansive clubhouses spanning over 50,000 sq. ft. each, along with an ultra-premium clubhouse of approximately 75,000 sq. ft.

These clubhouses are equipped with a wide range of high-end facilities such as rooftop barbecue areas, indoor games rooms, and stylish lounges. Residents can enjoy table tennis, billiards, card rooms, and recreational spaces within these hubs.

Additional amenities include a fully equipped gym, indoor swimming pool, aerobics and dance studios, spa and wellness centers, mini theatre, banquet halls, and party spaces. Convenience features like a supermarket, guest rooms, salon, and changing areas further enhance everyday comfort.

Dedicated Facilities for Children

Sobha One World ensures that children have access to safe and fun-filled spaces designed for both play and learning. Kids can enjoy specially designed play areas with swings, sand pits, trampolines, and climbing structures.

Adventure features such as rock climbing walls and mound slides add excitement, while interactive floor games and informal skating zones keep children engaged. Educational play elements like a giant abacus also encourage learning through fun activities.

Safety & Sustainable Living

The project places strong emphasis on safety and eco-friendly living. Sobha One World is equipped with 24/7 security personnel, CCTV surveillance across common areas, and controlled access points to ensure a secure environment for all residents.

Sustainability features include rainwater harvesting systems, solar-powered lighting, energy-efficient infrastructure, and proper waste management practices such as segregation and sewage treatment plants.

Additional conveniences like spacious parking areas, EV charging stations, and air-filling points support modern urban living while promoting environmentally responsible practices.

A Vibrant Community Lifestyle

Residents can also enjoy community-focused features such as outdoor movie screenings, landscaped party lawns, and dedicated game zones. These elements create a lively and interactive environment where neighbors can connect and build lasting relationships.

Expert Opinion on Sobha One World Amenities

Real estate experts have rated Sobha One World highly, with an average score of around 4.7 out of 5 for its amenities and overall planning. The project is widely appreciated for its modern facilities, focus on wellness, and strong potential for long-term value appreciation.

With its combination of luxury, functionality, and thoughtful design, Sobha One World stands out as one of the most sought-after residential developments in Hoskote, offering a complete lifestyle solution for modern homebuyers.

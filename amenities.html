Sobha One World Amenities

Sobha One World features an impressive collection of 55+ premium lifestyle amenities thoughtfully distributed across a beautifully planned 48-acre phase. Residents can enjoy a wide range of facilities including a grand clubhouse, multiple swimming pools, sports courts, landscaped gardens, and dedicated organic farming spaces. The development also integrates a retail plaza and modern co-working zones along Old Madras Road, making everyday living convenient without needing to step outside the township. This makes it one of the most sought-after residential options in Hoskote.

The project consists of 18 high-rise residential towers designed with three basement parking levels, a ground floor, and 53 upper floors, accommodating a total of 5,406 apartments. Homebuyers can choose from 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK configurations, with apartment sizes ranging from approximately 740 sq. ft. to 2,500 sq. ft.. With a strong focus on comfort, recreation, and safety, Sobha One World creates a well-balanced living environment that supports both active and peaceful lifestyles for its residents.

Recreational Zone

The recreational zone at Sobha One World is thoughtfully designed to offer residents a relaxing and enjoyable environment where they can unwind and spend meaningful time with family and friends. This zone blends leisure with wellness, creating a perfect escape within the township itself.

Residents can engage in a variety of indoor and outdoor activities such as board games, snooker, and a professionally designed squash court. For those seeking relaxation and rejuvenation, facilities like a spa, sauna, jacuzzi, and dedicated meditation pads provide a peaceful retreat. The area also features a beautifully designed clubhouse, a bio pond that enhances the natural ambiance, and a bonfire court for social gatherings and evening relaxation. In addition, multiple outdoor activity courts are available for recreational sports and casual play.

Sports & Fitness

The project places a strong emphasis on an active and healthy lifestyle by offering a wide range of sports and fitness amenities. Tree-lined jogging and walking tracks run across the development, encouraging daily exercise in a refreshing green environment.

For sports enthusiasts, there are dedicated facilities such as a golf putting zone, cricket practice nets, and multi-purpose sports courts suitable for games like basketball and beach volleyball. Children and adults alike can enjoy open playgrounds and cycling paths, while outdoor exercise decks and a skating rink add to the variety of physical activities available. The presence of a large outdoor swimming pool further enhances the fitness experience, making it easy for residents to stay active without leaving the community.

Lifestyle Amenities

Sobha One World offers a vibrant lifestyle through its thoughtfully curated amenities that cater to all age groups. Residents can enjoy courts for volleyball, tennis, and futsal, along with unique features like urban farming spaces and community gardens that promote sustainable living.

The township also includes well-designed gathering spaces such as poolside pavilions and open seating areas where residents can socialize and relax. Premium features like an infinity-edge pool, dedicated pet park, stylish cabanas, and themed activity zones like a giant chess board and adventure park add to the luxury experience. Skate parks and children’s play zones further ensure that every member of the family has access to engaging recreational options.

Clubhouse

The clubhouse facilities at Sobha One World are among the highlights of the project, offering a resort-like experience within the residential community. The development features two expansive clubhouses, each spanning over 50,000 sq. ft., along with an ultra-luxury clubhouse of approximately 75,000 sq. ft. designed with a grand double-height lobby.

These clubhouses are equipped with a wide range of premium amenities, including rooftop BBQ areas and pavilions for social gatherings. Indoor entertainment options such as table tennis, billiards, card rooms, and carrom rooms are available for leisure activities. Additional conveniences include a supermarket, guest rooms, salon, and changing facilities.

Residents can also enjoy modern fitness and wellness amenities like an aerobics studio, fully equipped gym, indoor swimming pool, dance studios, spa, and relaxation areas. Banquet halls, party spaces, and a mini theatre make the clubhouse a complete lifestyle destination.

Kids’ Facilities

Special attention has been given to children’s development and recreation with a wide range of dedicated play areas. These include safe and engaging spaces equipped with swings, sand pits, trampolines, and interactive play equipment.

Adventure-based features such as rock climbing walls and mound slides encourage physical activity, while interactive floor games and informal skating areas provide fun learning experiences. Unique additions like a giant abacus help combine play with educational engagement, ensuring a well-rounded environment for kids.

Safety & Sustainability

Sobha One World prioritizes both safety and environmental responsibility. The entire township is secured with 24/7 trained security personnel and CCTV surveillance across all common areas, ensuring a safe and peaceful living environment.

The project also incorporates modern infrastructure such as spacious parking areas with EV charging points and air-filling stations. Sustainable features include rainwater harvesting systems, solar-powered lighting, and energy-efficient fixtures to reduce environmental impact.

Proper waste segregation systems and a sewage treatment plant are implemented to maintain cleanliness and ecological balance within the community. Vehicle-free zones and pedestrian-friendly pathways further enhance safety and promote a healthier lifestyle.

Residents can also enjoy community-driven experiences like outdoor movie screenings, landscaped party lawns, and interactive game pods, making the township lively and socially engaging.

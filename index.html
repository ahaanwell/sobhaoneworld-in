Sobha One World is a modern, premium residential township developed by Sobha Limited in the rapidly growing locality of Hoskote, East Bangalore. Spread across an expansive 300-acre land parcel, this large-scale development is planned as an integrated community designed for comfortable and upscale living. The first phase, known as Sobha One Residences, covers approximately 48 acres and sets the foundation for this landmark project.

The development features 18 high-rise towers designed with a structure of 53 floors (3B+G+53). In total, the project will offer around 5,406 thoughtfully designed apartments available in 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK configurations. These homes are planned to suit a wide range of buyers, from young professionals to large families, with sizes ranging between 740 sq. ft. and 2,500 sq. ft. The starting price is around ₹1.09 Crores, and the official launch is expected in May 2026.

One of the key advantages of Sobha One World location . Situated in Sarakariguttahalli, Hoskote, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to important parts of East Bangalore. It is located just a few minutes from NH 75 and has easy access to major roads like NH 648 and SH 35. This strong road network ensures smooth travel to nearby IT hubs such as Whitefield, Varthur, and Marathahalli, making it an attractive choice for working professionals.

Hoskote itself is emerging as a preferred residential destination due to its balance of affordability and growth potential. Compared to more crowded city areas, it offers wider roads, cleaner surroundings, and a more peaceful living environment. At the same time, its proximity to key employment zones continues to drive demand for quality housing in the area, especially from reputed developers.

The project is also close to essential daily conveniences. Educational institutions like OM Shree Public School are located within a short distance, while healthcare facilities such as Ashwini Hospital are easily accessible. In addition, the upcoming Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR), located just a few minutes away, is expected to significantly improve connectivity and reduce travel time across the city.

Sobha One World is designed around the concept of a “5-minute city,” where essential services, daily needs, and lifestyle conveniences are easily reachable. Residents can expect manageable commute times to major tech corridors, with Whitefield approximately 12–13 km away, Varthur Road around 16 km, and Marathahalli close to 18 km. This makes it a practical choice for those looking to balance work and personal life.

Public transport connectivity further enhances the appeal of the project. The Kadugodi Metro Station on the Purple Line is located within a short driving distance, offering a reliable and traffic-free commuting option. Future metro expansions in this corridor are expected to boost accessibility even further, contributing to property value growth over time.

For frequent travelers, Kempegowda International Airport is about 39 km away and can be reached in under an hour via well-connected highways. This makes the project suitable not only for end users but also for investors looking for long-term appreciation and rental potential.

Buyers can submit their EOI through online payment or by issuing a post-dated cheque (PDC), which ensures priority during the allotment process.

Allotment Process (Step-by-Step)

  • EOI Submission (From May 10, 2026)
  • Buyers can submit their application by selecting their preferred unit, tower, floor, and facing.
  • Priority Allotment Window (Around May 20, 2026)
  • During this stage, applicants who submitted EOIs are given the first opportunity to choose units based on the order of submission.
  • Booking Confirmation
  • Once a unit is finalized, the EOI amount is adjusted towards the initial booking payment (typically 10% of the property value), confirming the purchase.

Early participants in the EOI phase can benefit from lower launch prices and better inventory options, which are expected to become limited after the official launch.

Sobha One World Master Plan

The master plan of Sobha One World is thoughtfully laid out across an expansive 300-acre land parcel, making it one of the largest integrated developments in East Bangalore. The project features 18 striking high-rise towers designed to stand out in the skyline, positioning it among the tallest township communities in the region.

With over 5,400 premium residences, the layout emphasizes both privacy and openness. The towers are strategically positioned to offer wide-angle views—up to 270 degrees—allowing residents to enjoy panoramic cityscapes along with ample natural light and ventilation.

At the heart of the township lies a centrally located clubhouse, serving as the main social and recreational hub for the community. This central placement ensures easy accessibility from all towers while creating a vibrant lifestyle zone within the project.

A key highlight of the planning is its strong focus on greenery and open space. Nearly 70% of the total land area is dedicated to landscaped gardens, open zones, and outdoor amenities, offering residents a calm, spacious, and nature-connected living environment.

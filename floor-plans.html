Sobha One World Floor Plans

Sobha One World offers intelligently planned floor layouts featuring 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments, with sizes ranging from approximately 650 sq. ft. to 2,495 sq. ft. Located within a vast 300-acre township, Phase 1 includes 18 high-rise towers built in a 3 basement + ground + 54-floor structure, accommodating nearly 5,400 residences.

Each apartment is designed with practical configurations such as 1 Bed 1 Bath, 2 Bed 2 Bath, 3 Bed 3 Bath, and 4 Bed 4 Bath, ensuring efficient use of space. The layouts include well-planned kitchens with attached utility areas and thoughtfully arranged living spaces that enhance comfort and functionality.

The design focuses on maximizing natural light, cross ventilation, and privacy between units. A podium-level concept places lifestyle amenities above dedicated parking areas, creating a safe and open environment for residents. With more than 65% open space across the project and a large 75,000 sq. ft. clubhouse, the development promotes a spacious, airy, and modern living experience.

Sobha One World Floor Plan – Detailed Overview

Sobha One World presents a thoughtfully designed range of floor plans that cater to modern urban lifestyles. The project offers premium 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments with sizes ranging from approximately 650 sq. ft. to 2495 sq. ft., ensuring options for individuals, couples, and large families alike. These homes are part of a massive 300-acre integrated township, featuring 18 iconic high-rise towers with a structure of 3 basements + ground + 54 floors, accommodating around 5,400 apartments.

Each layout has been carefully crafted to maximize usable space, natural lighting, ventilation, and privacy, while also maintaining a seamless connection between living, dining, and utility areas. The inclusion of podium-level amenities above dedicated parking zones ensures that residents enjoy a clutter-free and pedestrian-friendly environment with over 65% open spaces and access to a grand 75,000 sq. ft. clubhouse.

Premium Living Experience – “Sky Mansion” Concept

For end-users planning long-term living, especially by the expected possession timeline of 2031, the higher-floor residences (above the 30th level) stand out as the most desirable. These premium units are often referred to as “Sky Mansions”, offering expansive 180-degree panoramic views of the surrounding greenery and cityscape of Hoskote.

Positioned within premium tower stacks, the 3 BHK large units (with maid’s room) and 4 BHK residences deliver a villa-like experience in the sky. These homes are designed with wide frontage, enhanced airflow, and minimal noise disturbance, ensuring a calm and luxurious living environment. The elevation advantage also improves privacy and brings in abundant daylight throughout the day.

1 BHK Floor Plan – Smart and Efficient Living

The 1 BHK apartments at Sobha One World are designed with a built-up area of approximately 740 sq. ft., making them ideal for working professionals, first-time buyers, and investors.

  • A well-planned kitchen with attached utility space
  • A combined living and dining area for seamless usage
  • A spacious master bedroom
  • A dedicated entry foyer for added privacy
  • One wide balcony for natural light and ventilation
  • One attached bathroom

One of the standout features of the 1 BHK design is the private entry foyer, which prevents direct visibility into the living room from the main door. The living area opens into a balcony that enhances airflow and daylight. The overall layout follows a zero-wastage concept, ensuring that no area is lost to unnecessary passages or unused corners.

2 BHK Floor Plan – Balanced Comfort for Families

The 2 BHK units range between 1150 sq. ft. and 1350 sq. ft., offering a comfortable living space for nuclear families.

  • A modern kitchen with an attached utility
  • A large living and dining space
  • A master bedroom with attached bathroom
  • A second bedroom for guests or children
  • One or two balconies depending on the layout
  • One or two bathrooms

In most configurations, the living room connects to the main balcony, which often faces landscaped gardens or internal amenities. Bedrooms are thoughtfully positioned away from the entrance, creating a private sleeping zone that ensures peace and comfort within the home.

3 BHK Floor Plan – Spacious Homes for Growing Families

The 3 BHK apartments are designed for larger families and offer sizes ranging from 1445 sq. ft. to 1890 sq. ft. These homes combine space with functionality and luxury.

  • Three well-sized bedrooms
  • Three bathrooms
  • A spacious living and dining area
  • A kitchen with utility space
  • Two or three balconies

Many 3 BHK layouts provide flexibility, where one bedroom can be used as a guest room, study, or home office. Corner units are especially beneficial, as they have windows on multiple sides, allowing better airflow and increased natural lighting throughout the day.

4 BHK Floor Plan – Premium Luxury Living

The 4 BHK apartments are the most spacious units in the project, ranging from 1915 sq. ft. to 2495 sq. ft., and are designed for families seeking a high-end lifestyle.

  • Four large bedrooms
  • Four bathrooms
  • A grand living and dining space
  • A kitchen with utility area
  • Three or four balconies

In premium configurations, one bedroom is often placed slightly apart from the others, making it suitable for use as a parents’ suite, guest room, or private workspace. Large balconies provide extended outdoor space and offer panoramic views of the township, greenery, and skyline.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the total size of Sobha One World and where is it located?

Sobha One World is a large-scale integrated township spread across approximately 300 acres. The project is strategically located in Hoskote, East Bangalore, offering excellent connectivity to key areas like Whitefield, KR Puram, and major highways.

2. What types of apartments are available and what is the size range?

The project offers a wide range of 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK luxury apartments designed to suit different family needs. The apartment sizes typically range from around 740 sq. ft. to 2,425 sq. ft., providing options from compact homes to spacious premium residences.

3. What is meant by “Zero Dead Space” in the floor plan design?

The “Zero Dead Space” concept refers to a smart architectural approach where every part of the apartment is fully usable. This design avoids unnecessary corridors, awkward corners, or wasted areas, ensuring better space efficiency and functional layouts.

4. Do the apartments have proper ventilation and natural lighting?

Yes, all units are designed to ensure maximum airflow and natural light. The towers are placed at adequate distances from each other, and the layouts are planned in a way that allows fresh air circulation and sunlight to enter every home throughout the day.

5. Are the homes designed according to Vaastu principles?

Yes, the floor plans at Sobha One World are Vaastu-compliant, aligning with traditional design principles to create a balanced and positive living environment for residents.

6. When will the model apartments be available for site visits?

The model apartments for all configurations—1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK—are expected to be ready for viewing around the official launch in May 2026. Buyers will be able to visit the site and experience the layouts and finishes in person.

7. Do all apartments come with balconies?

Yes, every unit includes at least one spacious balcony. Larger configurations, especially 3 and 4 BHK Luxe variants, come with multiple balconies (2 to 4), offering extended outdoor space and better views of the surroundings.

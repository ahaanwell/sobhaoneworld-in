Apartment Booking Procedure

Once the project is officially launched, buyers can book units ranging from 1 BHK to premium 4+ BHK apartments. The booking process is simple and transparent:

  • Choose your preferred apartment configuration
  • Complete the booking application form
  • Pay approximately 10% of the property value as the initial booking amount
  • Submit KYC documents (Aadhaar card, PAN card, address proof, and photographs)
  • Coordinate with the sales team for further documentation and payment steps

Note: Submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) form early can give buyers priority access to select units.

Floor Plan Assistance

Sobha One World offers well-designed apartment layouts to suit different needs. Buyers can request detailed floor plan PDFs for various configurations such as 2, 3, and 4 BHK units.

The sales team will help you choose the ideal apartment based on:

  • Budget and investment goals
  • Preferred size and layout
  • Direction, facing, and view options

Apartment Allotment

After completing the booking process and payment, an official allotment letter is issued by the developer. This document includes complete details of the selected unit.

Allotment confirmation is typically shared:

  • Via email
  • Through WhatsApp
  • Within 24 hours of booking

Book a Site Visit

During the visit, the Sobha One World team will:

  • Provide a guided walkthrough of the project site
  • Explain layout plans and amenities

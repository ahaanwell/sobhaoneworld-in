Hoskote

Hoskote has quickly transformed into one of the most promising real estate destinations in East Bangalore in 2026. Located along NH-75 (PIN: 562114), the area has witnessed strong price growth—around 17.4% in the past year and over 115% in the last five years. Despite this sharp rise, property prices here are still approximately 30–40% lower than in established locations like Whitefield.

Its strategic location and upcoming infrastructure projects have made Hoskote a preferred choice for both homebuyers and investors.

Key Location Advantages

Hoskote benefits from proximity to major infrastructure developments, including:

Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR)
Bangalore–Chennai Expressway
Proposed KR Puram–Hoskote Metro Line

These projects significantly improve connectivity to key areas such as:

Whitefield (~15 km)
KR Puram (~30 km)
Kempegowda International Airport (~39 km)

With reputed developers like Sobha and Godrej launching large-scale projects, the region is rapidly evolving into a premium residential zone.

Neighbourhood Overview

Hoskote is part of the Bangalore Rural District and is governed by the local municipal authority. The area includes several key localities such as Kasaba, Jadigenahalli, Anugondanahalli, Nandagudi, and Sulibele.

Positioned at the junction of NH-75 and NH-648, Hoskote offers strong connectivity while avoiding heavy city congestion. The nearby industrial hub—just 7 km away—hosts major companies like Volvo and Honda, generating consistent housing demand.

An important aspect for buyers is that large residential developments in this region require BMRDA approval, offering an added layer of legal assurance.

Real Estate Growth Story

Hoskote’s journey from a semi-rural town to a real estate hotspot has been driven by affordability and proximity to employment hubs. Initially attracting buyers due to lower prices, the area soon gained popularity among IT professionals working in Whitefield.

As demand increased, major developers entered the market, launching large residential townships. Today, Hoskote offers a combination of affordability, connectivity, and long-term growth potential.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the average flat price in Hoskote in 2026?

Apartment prices in Hoskote typically range from ₹50 Lakhs to ₹1.9 Crores, depending on the project and configuration. Premium developments like Sobha OneWorld start from around ₹1.09 Crores, making the area more affordable compared to Whitefield.

2. How much have property prices increased in recent years?

Hoskote has seen strong price appreciation, with apartment values rising by over 115% in the past five years. In the last year alone, prices have increased by approximately 17%, while land prices have also grown significantly.

3. How far is Hoskote from Whitefield?

Hoskote is located around 15 km from Whitefield. The travel time is typically 20–25 minutes via NH-648, making it a convenient option for daily commuters.

4. Does Hoskote have metro connectivity?

Currently, the nearest metro access is the Kadugodi station on the Purple Line, which is about 15–20 minutes away. A proposed metro extension directly to Hoskote is also under planning.

5. What major infrastructure projects are nearby?

The area is supported by several key infrastructure developments, including the Bangalore–Chennai Expressway, the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR), and the upcoming Peripheral Ring Road (PRR), all of which will improve connectivity.

6. Is it a good time to invest in Hoskote?

Hoskote is considered a strong investment option, especially for long-term buyers. With relatively lower entry prices and multiple infrastructure projects underway, the area has good potential for future appreciation.

7. Are schools and hospitals easily accessible?

Yes, Hoskote has access to essential facilities such as OM Shree Public School and Ashwini Hospital, along with other educational institutions, healthcare centers, and daily convenience stores.

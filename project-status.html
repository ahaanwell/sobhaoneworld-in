Sobha OneWorld Project Status

Sobha OneWorld Project Status

The project status of Sobha OneWorld gives buyers a clear understanding of how the development is progressing. It helps investors and homebuyers track timelines, evaluate construction stages, and plan their purchase decisions with confidence. Regular updates are shared through official channels so buyers can stay informed and align their financial planning accordingly.

As of 2026, Sobha OneWorld is currently in its pre-launch stage, with the official launch expected in May 2026. This large-scale township is being developed across 300 acres in Hoskote, East Bangalore, with Phase 1 covering approximately 48 acres.

Current Construction Status

At present, early-stage groundwork has begun at the site. Initial development activities are focused on preparing the land and setting up the infrastructure required for large-scale construction.

Ongoing Site Activities:
Boundary Development:
The site has been fully secured with boundary fencing, and controlled entry and exit points have been established.
Land Preparation:
Activities such as land clearing, leveling, and grading are currently in progress to prepare the base for construction.
Internal Planning:
Layout marking and internal road alignment are underway as per the master plan.
Temporary Infrastructure:
Site offices and support facilities have been set up to manage ongoing operations.

These early steps indicate that the project is moving forward as per schedule and is entering the initial phase of development.

Project Overview

Sobha OneWorld is a premium integrated township located in the rapidly developing Hoskote region. The project features modern high-rise towers planned to rise up to 54 floors, offering a mix of luxury and functional living.

Key Details:
Configuration: 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments
Size Range: 740 sq. ft. to 2,425 sq. ft.
Total Towers (Phase 1): 18
Total Units: Approximately 5,400

Detailed floor plans are currently being finalized, and model apartments are under development. These sample units will be available for site visits after the official launch, allowing buyers to experience the design and quality firsthand.

RERA Approval Status

Sobha Limited has already initiated the process for Karnataka RERA registration. The official RERA number is expected to be issued around May 2026.

Once approved:
All project details will be listed on the official RERA portal
Buyers will get access to verified construction updates
Legal transparency and compliance will be ensured

Interested buyers can also connect with the sales team for the latest updates regarding approvals and documentation.

Booking & Sales Status

The project is currently open for Expression of Interest (EOI), which allows buyers to indicate their preferred unit type and configuration in advance.

Current Sales Phase:
EOI process active (early 2026)
Unit selection available for 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK options
Official bookings to begin after launch in May 2026

This early stage provides an opportunity for buyers to secure preferred units before public sales begin.

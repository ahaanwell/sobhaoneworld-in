Sobha One World Price – Latest Pre-Launch Cost of 1, 2, 3 & 4 BHK Apartments

The Sobha One World price for premium apartments in Hoskote, East Bangalore, currently ranges between ₹1.09 Crores and ₹3.63 Crores, depending on the unit configuration, size, tower positioning, and floor level. As the project is in its pre-launch phase, these prices are introductory and offer a strong advantage for early investors and homebuyers.

The development spans a massive 300-acre integrated township with 18 proposed high-rise towers and offers spacious homes ranging from 740 sq. ft. to 2425 sq. ft. This wide range of unit sizes ensures flexibility for buyers with different budgets and lifestyle requirements.

At present, the pre-launch base price is approximately ₹14,7020 per sq. ft., which is estimated to be 8–12% lower than comparable luxury projects in Whitefield and nearby IT corridors. This pricing gap gives early buyers a clear entry advantage in one of East Bangalore’s fastest-growing real estate markets.

Sobha One World Payment Plan – Flexible & Buyer-Friendly Structure

The Sobha One World payment plan is designed to provide maximum flexibility and financial convenience for homebuyers and investors. It clearly outlines the installment schedule, payment milestones, and timelines, ensuring complete transparency throughout the buying journey.

Buyers can take advantage of risk-managed installment options, where payments are aligned with construction progress. This reduces financial burden and makes it easier to plan long-term investments in a premium township project in East Bangalore.

At the time of booking, buyers are required to pay 10% of the total apartment value, which confirms the unit. The remaining amount is distributed across multiple stages linked to project development milestones.

Note: Registration charges, stamp duty, and government fees are not included in the base price and must be paid separately at the time of registration.

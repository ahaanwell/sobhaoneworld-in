Sobha One World Price

The pricing of apartments at Sobha One World reflects its positioning as a large-scale, premium residential township in East Bangalore. Designed by Sobha Limited, this project offers luxury living within a thoughtfully planned 300-acre development, making it one of the most anticipated real estate launches in the region.

As of the pre-launch phase, the starting price for apartments begins from approximately ₹1.09 Crores. However, the exact pricing varies based on multiple factors such as apartment configuration, floor level, tower positioning, view, and overall unit size.

Since the project is currently in its early stage, buyers entering during this phase can benefit from lower introductory prices before the official launch. This makes it an attractive opportunity for both end-users and investors looking for long-term appreciation.

The project is strategically located opposite Hoskote Toll Plaza on Old Madras Road (NH 75), ensuring excellent connectivity to key areas like Whitefield, KR Puram, and the upcoming infrastructure corridors.

Sobha One World Pre-Launch Price List (Detailed Overview)

Below is an approximate overview of apartment configurations along with their super built-up areas:

  • 1 BHK Apartments – Around 740 sq. ft.
  • 2 BHK Apartments (Compact) – Around 1,070 sq. ft.
  • 2 BHK Apartments (Large) – Around 1,200 sq. ft.
  • 3 BHK Luxury Units – Around 1,525 sq. ft.
  • 3 BHK Grande (with 2 car parks) – Around 1,825 sq. ft.
  • 4 BHK Luxury Units (with 2 car parks) – Around 2,100 sq. ft.
  • 4 BHK Grande Apartments (with 2 car parks) – Around 2,500 sq. ft.

These sizes are designed to cater to a wide range of buyers, from young professionals to large families seeking spacious and premium homes.

Important Note:

The prices mentioned during pre-launch are base prices only. The final cost of an apartment may increase depending on:

  • Preferred floor height
  • Tower location within the township
  • Facing/view (garden, clubhouse, road, etc.)
  • Demand during launch phases

Additional Costs Buyers Should Know

Many buyers often assume that the quoted price is the final amount, but that is not the case. Several mandatory charges are applicable during the purchase process.

These include:

  • Goods and Services Tax (GST)
  • Stamp duty charges
  • Registration fees
  • Legal and documentation charges
  • Maintenance deposits
  • Clubhouse membership fees (if applicable)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) – Sobha One World Price

1. What is the starting price of apartments at Sobha One World?

The starting price of apartments at Sobha One World is approximately ₹1.09 Crores. However, this is the base price for entry-level units, and the final cost depends on factors such as the apartment configuration (1, 2, 3, or 4 BHK), total area, tower location, and floor level. Premium units with better views or higher floors are usually priced higher.

2. Are GST and other charges included in the apartment price?

No, the quoted price is only the base cost of the apartment. Additional charges such as GST, stamp duty, registration fees, and other legal or administrative costs are not included in this amount. Buyers should consider these extra expenses while planning their total investment, as they can significantly impact the overall budget.

3. Are there any benefits for early buyers in Sobha One World?

Yes, buyers who invest during the pre-launch stage can take advantage of lower introductory pricing compared to later phases. Early buyers also get better options in terms of preferred towers, floor selection, and unit views. Additionally, pre-launch investors often benefit from higher price appreciation as the project progresses.

4. What is the Expression of Interest (EOI) amount for booking an apartment?

To reserve a unit during the pre-launch phase, buyers are required to pay an Expression of Interest (EOI) amount. The approximate EOI amounts are:

  • 2 BHK apartments: ₹5 Lakhs
  • 3 BHK apartments: ₹8 Lakhs
  • 4 BHK apartments: ₹10 Lakhs

This amount helps buyers secure their preferred unit before the official launch and high demand.

5. Does the apartment price change based on floor, tower, or view?

Yes, the final price of an apartment can vary depending on several factors. Units located on higher floors, in premium towers, or offering better views such as garden-facing or clubhouse-facing typically come at a higher price. Demand, location within the township, and overall unit positioning all play a role in determining the final cost.

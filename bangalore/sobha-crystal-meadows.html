Sobha Crystal Meadows – Row Houses on Sarjapur Road

Sobha Crystal Meadows is a low-rise row house community on Sarjapur Road, one of Bengaluru's most established IT-adjacent corridors. Unlike Sobha Limited's typical high-rise apartment format, this project is built around independent 4 BHK row homes spread across a 26-acre footprint, rolled out in 5 phases. The project has already been launched, with 290 units on offer and possession slated for 2029.

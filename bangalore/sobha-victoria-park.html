Sobha Victoria Park – Apartments Off Hennur Road

Sobha Victoria Park is a more compact, mid-scale development compared to Sobha Group's larger townships, set off Hennur Road on a 6.03-acre plot. It is currently under construction, with 5 towers of 9 floors each holding 300 apartments in 2 and 3 BHK layouts ranging from 865 to 1,562 sq.ft. Hennur Road has become a steady residential corridor linking North and Northeast Bangalore, and this project is priced from ₹1.29 Cr, with possession targeted for December 2027.
